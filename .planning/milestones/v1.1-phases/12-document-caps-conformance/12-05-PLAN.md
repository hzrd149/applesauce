<objective>
Change what bounds the Community List: out with every serialized-byte cap, in with the
50-membership protocol constant. Delete `COMMUNITY_LIST_MAX_ENTRY_BYTES` with its derivation
comment and its `recordJoin` throw, delete `communityListWithinByteCap`, drop
`saveCommunityList`'s refusal while keeping its diagnostic, and add the live-membership count cap
at `recordJoin` — the one enforcement point, with merged overflow deliberately tolerated.

Purpose: audit finding M12. CORD-02 §8 states "The List caps at 50 memberships... the cap is a
protocol constant, not client taste", and the code implements the byte cap (the explicit MUST) but
not the count. D-07 removes the byte caps because their premise moved upstream, which makes the
50-membership count the *only* remaining bound on this document — strengthening rather than
weakening the case for it. D-06 fixes the counting rule (live only, per the spec's own word
"memberships") and the enforcement asymmetry (refuse local, tolerate merged), mirroring the
documented asymmetry the byte ceiling already used.

Output: `helpers/community-list.ts` with the entry ceiling gone and a new membership constant,
`client/client.ts` with `recordJoin`'s guard swapped and `saveCommunityList`'s refusal dropped, and
rewritten tests carrying the new behavior.
</objective>
