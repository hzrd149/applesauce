across four files.

proofs in one new suite, while its own per-task verification map gives WIRE-12 the command filter
`-- citations` and WIRE-09 the filter `-- document-caps-conformance`. This plan follows the
per-task map: the citation guard gets `__tests__/cord-citations.test.ts` and plan 12-09's
client-tier round-trip proofs get `__tests__/document-caps-conformance.test.ts`. Two sibling suites
rather than one satisfies the Wave 0 intent (cross-cutting proofs get their own new suites rather
