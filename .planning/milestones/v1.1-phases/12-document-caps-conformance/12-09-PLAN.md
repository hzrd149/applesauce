and the phase's cross-cutting conformance suite.

Note on the carrier and D-12's rejection: D-12 rejected a bolted-on carrier field at the PARSE
layer, because there the root itself could be opened and the reconstruction deleted — a carrier
would have kept the reconstruction. At the CLIENT tier the situation is different: the engine's
state is genuinely a reduced projection (two merged arrays plus derived engines), the document is
not held in memory at all, and there is no reconstruction to delete because the arrays are the
authoritative merged state. A carrier is the structural fix here, not a patch around one. Record
this distinction in the code comment so a future reader does not cite D-12 to remove it.
