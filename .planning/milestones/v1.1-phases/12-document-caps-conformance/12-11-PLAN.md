<objective>
Prove the downstream half of **CR-01**'s closure: that a channel folded from an edition carrying a non-boolean `deleted` is not merely well-shaped, but is actually reachable through the three `client/community.ts` gates that made CR-01 a live defect rather than a type-hygiene complaint.

12-VERIFICATION.md's closure contract names this explicitly — the regression test must assert "the channel remains reachable via `publicChannelKeys()` / is not silently dead". Plan 12-10 closes the fold; a fold-level assertion alone would still leave the claim "an authorized `MANAGE_CHANNELS` holder can no longer make a channel visible-but-silently-dead" resting on inference across a module boundary. `publicChannelKeys()` (`:757`), `reconcileLive`'s `publicIds` (`:807`) and `reconcilePrivateChannels` (`:830`) each hold their own copy of a `!c.deleted` predicate, and `channels$` (`:414-424`) holds none — the divergence between those four is what produced a channel that renders while never syncing.

Purpose: pin the end-to-end behavior at the boundary a user would actually feel, with discriminating controls so the assertions cannot pass on a build that simply stopped honoring `deleted` at all, and with a mandatory revert-and-observe probe so neither test can be vacuous (the WR-08 anti-pattern this phase exists to eliminate).

Output: two behavioral tests inside `community.test.ts`'s existing `wire conformance` describe, plus a recorded non-vacuity transcript.

**Design note, stated rather than left implicit.** The obvious alternative — tighten the three gates to `c.deleted === true` — is deliberately NOT taken. It would move the guarantee from the fold (one place, governing every present and future consumer) into three call sites that can drift apart again, which is the same enumerated-patch shape this phase has now been bitten by twice. The fold-level invariant established by 12-10 is that `deleted` on a folded `ChannelMetadata` is boolean-or-absent, which makes all three gates correct as written. These tests exist to prove that invariant reaches them.
</objective>
