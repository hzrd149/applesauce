<objective>
Close CR-03 by applying to `count()` the exact shape plan 13-09 applied to `req()`, then complete the
sweep by auditing every remaining auth-reachable call site against the invariant.

**Structural choice (stated explicitly per planning directive 1):** the invariant is the one 13-05
discovered and 13-09 generalised — *nothing that completes on the auth-required signal is hoisted above
the retry boundary; the send side effect and the terminating listen chain are constructed together, per
attempt, inside one unshared `defer`.* `count()` already splits send from listen, which is why the
verifier saw its resend reach the wire; what it does not do is give that resend a live listen chain. Its
`messages` is `share()`d at call scope and completes inclusively on the auth-required signal
(relay.ts:986), so the second COUNT joins a stream that is already terminating: the verifier observed
`["COUNT","COUNT","CLOSE","CLOSE"]`, a completion with zero values, and a genuine COUNT reply sent 50ms
later never observed.

**Planning directive 2 — propagate the defect class backward, not just forward.** 13-05 fixed this class
for `event()` alone and it was never carried to `req()` or `count()`; that omission is the entire reason
this gap-closure phase exists. Task 3 therefore audits all eight auth-reachable operations against the
invariant and records a verdict per site, so the class is swept rather than chased one instance at a
time. That includes the operations reachable only through `RelayGroup` and `RelayPool`, which wrap these
same methods.

Purpose: RAUTH-03 fails on `count()` and RAUTH-07 cannot hold while any of the eight sites is
unverified. A zero-value completion is worse than an error here — `firstValueFrom(relay.count(...))`
rejects with `EmptyError` instead of returning a count, and `RelayGroup.count`'s `combineLatest` never
emits for that relay at all.
Output: a `count()` matching the uniform shape, COUNT-side non-vacuity tests, and a recorded audit
verdict for every site.
</objective>
