<objective>
Remove the standing "am I authenticated" surface (D-10) and its relay-wide-flag gate (D-11) from
every layer that carries it: `ConcordCommunity.authenticated$`, `ConcordPrivateChannel.authenticated$`,
the `authenticated` member of all THREE status types (including `ConcordClientStatus`, which
RESEARCH.md found is not named in CONTEXT.md's canonical_refs), the `authenticated` leg of both
`status$` composites and their `distinctUntilChanged` comparators, `ConcordClient`'s fold over
child `.authenticated`, and the two example badges that render it.

At the same time re-home `connected$` (D-12) onto the `connectedRelays$` free function created in
plan 15-01, so neither engine calls the class scheduled for deletion for its connection state.

Purpose: `authenticated$`'s old definition is unsatisfiable under per-operation auth in two
independent ways (D-11) — its gate reads relay-wide flags that any other scope's refusal can set,
and it is an all-of check over the scope's entire key set while only the subset an operation
requested is ever authenticated. Both of CAUTH-03's flag readers inside `relay-auth.ts` die with
it. Doing this before the engine rewire keeps the engine plan (15-04) focused on the auth
mechanism alone.

Output: three status types one field lighter, both engines free of the deleted class's status
methods, and the examples still building.
</objective>
