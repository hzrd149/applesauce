<objective>
Close WR-01 and WR-02: two ways an auth problem in the engines is currently either
mis-registered or invisible.

**WR-01** (`community.ts:1220`, `:1556-1560`): both `rotateChannel()` and `refound()` recompute
the channel-rekey `GroupKey` from `this.material.community_root` re-read AFTER several awaits
(`admin.vacFor`, `buildChannelRekey` / `buildRefounding`, and in `refound` the whole root-roll
`requireMajority` loop), while the wraps being registered for were built from the snapshot
captured before those awaits. `checkRekey()` runs on a 200ms timer off the rekey plane and can
call `adoptRefounding()` in that window, reassigning `this.keys` and therefore
`this.material.community_root`. The registration then computes the wrong key and the very
publishes it exists to make answerable become unanswerable — aborting the whole Refounding on
a gating relay with a misleading "not confirmed by a majority of relays" error.

The verifier's stated remedy is to capture the root once before the first await. This plan
does something strictly stronger and structural instead, and the deviation must be recorded in
the SUMMARY: **the builders return the `GroupKey` they actually finalized the wraps with, and
the callers register that value.** A snapshot local still leaves two derivations of the same
value that a future edit can desync; carrying the key out of the builder makes the desync
unrepresentable. This is the same one-source principle plan 15-09 applied to `wrapForTarget`
on the plane-publish path.

**WR-02** (`community.ts:302`, `:359`, `:560`; `private-channel.ts:121`, `:173-177`, `:261`):
`authFailure` is a mutable field written by the holder's `onAuthFailure` callback at any time
but read exactly ONCE, at the end of `start()` / `walk()`. With `authenticated$` removed from
both engines and from `ConcordCommunityStatus` (D-10), an app now has NO signal that a relay is
rejecting its stream keys during steady-state operation. Replace the latched field with the
value stream D-13 already designated: push the message straight into the existing `error$`
`BehaviorSubject`, which is already a leg of the `status$` composite.

Purpose: WR-01 protects the registrations that make a Refounding publishable; WR-02 is what
makes plan 15-10's new zero-answer report (WR-03) actually reach a UI rather than a debug
namespace nobody has enabled.

Output: rekey registrations derived from the plan that produced the wraps, and auth failure as
a live value on both engines' existing error surface.
</objective>
