### Open pending todos (2, both `high`)

- **`05.1-review-followups.md`** — includes a **pre-existing security BLOCKER**: `getGiftWrapSeal` (`packages/common/src/helpers/gift-wrap.ts:209`) calls `verifyWrappedEvent(seal)` and discards the boolean, so an invalid-signature seal is accepted and cached; combined with the `rumor.pubkey === seal.pubkey` binding against an unverified `seal.pubkey`, this is an author-spoofing bypass. The concord sibling guards correctly. Not a v1.1 regression. Plus WR-01, WR-03, WR-04 and 4 INFO items.

Pre-existed for chat, but WIRE-02 changed it from *requires a deliberate user action* to *happens automatically* — a change in kind not covered by D-04's accepted trade-off.
