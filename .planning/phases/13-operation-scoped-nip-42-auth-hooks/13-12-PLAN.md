<objective>
Close RAUTH-08 and book the phase's requirement state.

RAUTH-08's threading was always genuine — `sync-loader.test.ts` already asserts call-argument
pass-through to both `request()` and `sync()`. Its two remaining gaps were both *downstream* of fixes
made earlier in this wave sequence, which is why this plan runs last:

1. The paginated path threads into `relay.request()`, which inherited `req()`'s unbounded-retry and
   dropped-resend defects. Plans 13-08 and 13-09 fixed those upstream; what is missing here is the
   loader-side assertion that the paginated path is bounded rather than looping.
2. D-16's "an auth-required failure on the negentropy path must not trigger the paginated fallback"
   guard (sync-loader.ts:592-608) is keyed on `RELAY_AUTH_ERROR_NAMES.has(error.name)`
   (sync-loader.ts:89). CR-04 let a synchronously-throwing handler escape as a raw `Error` whose `.name`
   is `"Error"`, so the guard silently under-matched and the loader fell back onto the paginated request
   against the same auth wall. Plan 13-08 Task 2 fixed the escape at the source; what is missing here is
   the loader-side assertion that the guard actually holds for that shape.

**Constraint on how this is tested:** `applesauce-loaders` deliberately depends only on
`applesauce-core`, `nanoid` and `rxjs` (D-06), and `applesauce-relay` is not even a devDependency. These
tests must therefore be *contract* tests against the loader's injected `request`/`sync` functions, not
end-to-end tests against a real `Relay`. The wire-level proof lives in plan 13-09's relay tests; this
plan proves the loader honours the contract those tests establish. **Do not add `applesauce-relay` as a
dependency or devDependency of `applesauce-loaders`.**

Purpose: three requirements were reopened because REQUIREMENTS.md recorded a gap-blind suite's green
result as verification. This plan closes the last behavioural gap and flips the status only after every
regression suite in the phase is green.
Output: two loader contract tests, one single-sentence changeset per distinct published fix, and a
requirement ledger that reflects verified behaviour.
</objective>
