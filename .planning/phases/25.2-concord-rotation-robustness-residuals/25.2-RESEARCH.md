## User Constraints (from CONTEXT.md)

### Locked Decisions

### Complete-Set Relay Coverage
- **D-01:** Before adopting a Refounding, one identical strict majority of configured protocol relays must acknowledge every root-roll chunk, every accompanying private-channel rekey wrap, and every Control compaction wrap. Independent per-wrap majorities are insufficient because their intersection may be empty.
- **D-02:** Extra transport relays receive rotation artifacts but never count toward the common-majority denominator or intersection. Preserve Phase 12.3's protocol-relay attribution and URL normalization rules.
- **D-03:** Each invocation attempts every prepared mandatory artifact exactly once, even if an earlier response already makes success impossible. Evaluate the common relay intersection only after collecting the complete acknowledgement matrix.
- **D-04:** A failed common-majority gate prevents local adoption and rejects the invocation. The new Guestbook membership snapshot remains best-effort as CORD-06 requires; its failure cannot undo an otherwise successful Refounding.
- **D-05:** Control compaction joins the mandatory common-majority gate. The Guestbook snapshot does not, but it is attempted once after successful adoption conditions are satisfied and its failure is surfaced as a warning.

### Durable, Idempotent Refounding Resume
- **D-06:** A retry republishes the exact same prepared and signed root chunks, channel rekeys, Control compaction, snapshot, keys, and correlation fields. It must not regenerate any artifact or mint a competing same-epoch candidate.
- **D-07:** Pending Refounding state survives process restart, including enough protected material to reproduce the exact publication attempt. This makes CORD-06's crashed-Refounder resume claim true rather than limiting resume to one in-memory client lifetime. — **Reversibility:** costly — relaxing durability later would strand persisted pending rotations and weaken an observable recovery guarantee.
- **D-08:** A new Refounding request for a community with a pending next-epoch Refounding automatically resumes that pending operation. It cannot create a second candidate while the pending record exists.
- **D-09:** Clear the pending record only after the mandatory publication gate succeeds, local adoption succeeds, and the best-effort Guestbook snapshot has been attempted. Retain it through every earlier failure.

### Immediate Live Convergence
- **D-10:** A complete, authorized, continuity-valid, strictly lower same-epoch rotation arriving through the live stream is evaluated through the shared convergence core and adopted immediately. Do not wait for a full or manually triggered sync.
- **D-11:** A partial live chunk set is retained without adoption or removal. Actively fetch its missing chunk indexes from configured protocol relays and reevaluate as relevant events arrive.
- **D-12:** Chunk sets are grouped and checked consistently across live and historical paths. Disagreement in `n`, `prevepoch`, continuity fields, or generation marks the set inconsistent; retain typed diagnostic evidence and fetch rather than allowing first arrival to define the set.
- **D-13:** Live convergence is down-only. A settled epoch may move only to a strictly lower valid sibling and must never move back upward.
- **D-14:** Converging to a lower sibling produces one coherent state transition. Rebuild the epoch walk, stores, subscriptions, Control fold, and Guestbook view before publishing the new state; consumers must not observe mixed roots. Retain both fork keys so losing-fork history remains readable.

### Honest Publication and Processing Outcomes
- **D-15:** A mandatory publication failure throws a typed error containing the stable pending-rotation identity, failed artifact identities, normalized per-relay acknowledgement evidence, and original causes. The pending operation remains available for resume. — **Reversibility:** costly — this establishes a caller-visible error contract and diagnostic schema.
- **D-16:** Successful `refound()` returns a structured success result containing the adopted epoch and structured warnings for best-effort failures such as Guestbook snapshot publication. Completion alone must not imply every non-gating publish succeeded. — **Reversibility:** one-way — changing the existing successful return surface requires a public API migration and downstream caller updates.
- **D-17:** Malformed, unauthorized, incomplete, citation-blocked, or temporarily undecryptable remote candidates emit typed diagnostics and do not terminate live rotation observation. A local persistence or transition failure that leaves rotation state unreliable terminates the stream.
- **D-18:** Do not swallow publication, parsing, decryption, citation, persistence, or convergence errors. Preserve safe typed classification and original causes while ensuring relay-controlled content cannot forge logs or leak secrets.

### Exact Rotation Authority Citations
- **D-19:** A non-owner rotation's `vac` must identify the actor's deterministic Grant coordinate, exact cited version, and exact edition content hash. Resolve and verify all three before the rotation becomes eligible for authority evaluation.
- **D-20:** The citation is a synchronization floor, not the authority verdict. After resolving it, evaluate the actor's permission and strict rank against the current refuse-downgrade roster; a newer demotion or revoke defeats an older valid citation.
- **D-21:** An unavailable cited Grant parks only that rotation while bounded targeted Control synchronization runs. It never blocks unrelated actions, rotations, rendering, or reads.
- **D-22:** A present version with a different hash remains parked while bounded reconciliation could resolve a gap or fork. Once the bound is exhausted, discard the candidate and emit a typed citation-mismatch diagnostic.
- **D-23:** Owner-authored rotations must omit `vac`. An owner rotation carrying a citation is a malformed non-canonical wire shape and is rejected.

## Summary

The current implementation already builds every signed Refounding artifact before publishing, uses normalized protocol relays for quorum attribution, groups chunks through one shared helper, and reuses the same down-only comparator in live and historical paths. It does not yet satisfy this phase because `refound()` publishes mandatory wraps sequentially and stops on the first failure, checks a separate majority per wrap rather than one common intersection, treats Control compaction as fire-and-forget, swallows snapshot errors, regenerates a new plan on retry, and returns `void`. [VERIFIED: codebase]

The receive path has the right convergence spine but insufficient orchestration. Root live processing reads a shared rekey store after a timer; channel live processing uses an in-memory map; neither path actively fetches a partial set, exact `vac` version/hash resolution is absent, and state adoption mutates keys/subscriptions/observables before a full lower-sibling rebuild is complete. [VERIFIED: codebase] CORD-06 requires complete chunk sets, resumable/idempotent publication, retained fork keys, and down-only healing; CORD-04 makes the exact three-part `vac` a synchronization floor while current roster state remains the authority verdict. [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/06.md] [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/04.md]

**Primary recommendation:** Add one rotation coordinator shared by root and channel paths, persist an encrypted prepared Refounding before its first publish, evaluate publication from a complete acknowledgement matrix, resolve citations asynchronously before the pure winner fold, and commit lower-sibling transitions behind a single state-publication barrier. [VERIFIED: codebase]

## Architecture Patterns

### System Architecture Diagram

```text
refound(opts)
  -> load encrypted pending record? -- yes --> decrypt + restore exact plan
             | no
             -> buildRefounding -> encrypt+persist prepared record
  -> publish EVERY mandatory artifact once -> normalized ack matrix
  -> intersect protocol-relay ack sets -> strict-majority gate
       | fail -> typed RefoundingPublicationError; retain pending
       | pass -> stage/adopt coherent epoch -> attempt snapshot once
              -> return RefoundingResult { epoch, rotationId, warnings }
              -> clear pending

live/sync rekey wrap -> parse/group/diagnose -> partial or citation parked?
  -> targeted protocol-relay fetch -> reevaluate shared convergence core
  -> authorized + complete + continuity-valid + strictly lower?
  -> staged epoch/channel rebuild -> persist -> one observable/subscription commit
```

### Pattern 1: Persist-Before-Publish Prepared Operation

Build and sign once, derive stable IDs from already signed event IDs, encrypt the serializable record to the user's own pubkey, and save it before the first relay call. A restored record must be schema-validated before decryption output is trusted. [VERIFIED: codebase] [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/06.md]

```typescript
// Proposed shape; uses existing signer/storage seams.
const pending = await pendingStore.load(cid);
const plan = pending ?? await prepareRefounding(opts);
if (!pending) await pendingStore.save(plan);
return publishPreparedRefounding(plan);
```

Use `rotationId = plan.rekeyWraps[0].id` and `artifactId = wrap.id`; both are stable, non-secret signed-event identifiers and require no extra hash scheme. [VERIFIED: codebase]

### Pattern 2: Complete Acknowledgement Matrix

Invoke every mandatory artifact publish exactly once, collect successes/failures with `Promise.allSettled`, normalize only response origins, and intersect per-artifact protocol-relay success sets. Extra relays remain publish targets but are filtered before the matrix. [VERIFIED: codebase]

```typescript
const settled = await Promise.allSettled(
  artifacts.map((a) => pool.publish(transport, a.wrap, publishOpts(a.wrap))),
);
const matrix = buildAckMatrix(artifacts, settled, protocolRelays);
const common = intersect(matrix.map((row) => row.acceptedProtocolRelays));
if (common.size <= protocolRelays.length / 2) throw failure(matrix);
```

### Pattern 3: Resolve Citation, Then Judge Current Authority

Treat exact edition resolution and authorization as separate stages. Missing/mismatch yields a candidate-local parked state and bounded fetch; a resolved pin proceeds to the current roster, where permission and strict-rank rules may still reject it. Owner-with-`vac` is malformed before either stage. [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/04.md]

### Pattern 4: Stage Then Commit a Lower Sibling

Do not call the current `adoptRefounding()` directly from a live timer. Run the shared epoch/channel walk into staged stores/state, retain old and winner keys, await material persistence, then swap keys, folds, subscriptions, epoch/status, and diagnostics in one ordered commit with outward observables suppressed until the staged snapshot is ready. [VERIFIED: codebase]

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Per-wrap majority:** `{A,B}`, `{B,C}`, `{A,C}` passes three local majorities while no relay holds the complete set. Use the common intersection. [VERIFIED: CONTEXT.md]
- **Regenerate on retry:** fresh random roots, timestamps, snapshot seeds, ciphertexts, and event IDs mint a competing same-epoch candidate. Restore signed bytes. [VERIFIED: codebase]
- **Persist plaintext plan:** `JoinMaterial`, group secret keys, and NIP-44 recipient ciphertext context are sensitive; raw `localStorage` is not protection. [VERIFIED: codebase] [CITED: https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/blob/v4.0.3/4.0/en/0x16-V8-Data-Protection.md]
- **Boolean authorization filter:** filtering invalid sets out of an array cannot explain parked, malformed, mismatched, or unauthorized outcomes. Preserve a typed candidate state. [VERIFIED: codebase]
- **Fire-and-forget store writes:** current routing can schedule rekey evaluation before an async store add resolves, and logs persistence failure without terminating the unreliable stream. Await the rotation-path write. [VERIFIED: codebase]
- **Second winner algorithm:** root/channel and live/historical paths must share group, continuity, authority, decryptability, and lowest-key decisions. [VERIFIED: phase 08 summaries]
- **Raw relay-controlled log text:** keep messages, ciphertext, `vac` values, keys, and relay error strings out of formatted operational logs; expose original causes on typed objects. [VERIFIED: codebase] [CITED: https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/blob/v4.0.3/4.0/en/0x15-V7-Error-Logging.md]

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Clearing Pending State Too Early
**What goes wrong:** A process crash after publication but before snapshot attempt loses the exact resume candidate.  
**How to avoid:** Persist state stages; remove only after mandatory gate, idempotent local adoption/persistence, and recorded snapshot attempt.  
**Warning signs:** `removeItem()` occurs in a `finally` block or before `RefoundingResult` construction.

### Pitfall 2: Resume Against Already-Adopted Material
**What goes wrong:** Restart loads the new material plus a pending record created under the prior epoch. Rebuilding from current state mints the wrong addresses or falsely treats the record as stale. [VERIFIED: codebase]  
**How to avoid:** Persist prior material/address keys and next material; if current material already equals the pending next root/epoch, skip the state mutation but continue the pending snapshot/cleanup stages.  
**Warning signs:** restored code calls `buildRefounding()` or recomputes `rekeyKey` from `this.material`.

### Pitfall 3: Partial Fetch Cannot Filter on Inner Chunk Tags
**What goes wrong:** Gift-wrap filters cannot target encrypted rumor `chunk` tags, so a supposed missing-index network filter is ineffective. [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/06.md]  
**How to avoid:** Fetch the known rekey author address from protocol relays, decode locally, then retain only events filling the candidate's missing indexes.  
**Warning signs:** a relay filter contains `#chunk` for kind 1059 wraps.

### Pitfall 4: Async Store Race
**What goes wrong:** `route()` currently fires an async add and schedules evaluation immediately, so a persistent store may not contain the new chunk when checked. [VERIFIED: codebase]  
**How to avoid:** Await/add-to-buffer first for rekey events, serialize coordinator runs, and reevaluate after completion.  
**Warning signs:** `void checkRekey()` or a timer is scheduled outside the store-add resolution.

### Pitfall 5: Owner Exemption Becomes Owner Citation Tolerance
**What goes wrong:** Current `vacVerifier` returns true for the owner even when a `vac` is present. [VERIFIED: codebase]  
**How to avoid:** Canonical-shape validation first: owner plus citation is rejected; only owner plus absent citation is exempt.  
**Warning signs:** owner check precedes `vac === undefined` enforcement.

### Pitfall 6: Exact Pin Grandfathers Old Authority
**What goes wrong:** Matching the historical cited Grant is mistaken for current permission.  
**How to avoid:** Exact pin only unblocks evaluation; use the current folded roster for bit and rank. [CITED: https://github.com/concord-protocol/concord/blob/main/04.md]  
**Warning signs:** authority is derived from cited edition content rather than `state.grants`/roles.

### Pitfall 7: Observable Mixed State
**What goes wrong:** Current adoption rewires state, opens live, emits epoch, triggers callbacks, and refreshes channels in separate steps. Consumers can see a new epoch with old folds or vice versa. [VERIFIED: codebase]  
**How to avoid:** Stage data and suppress outward emissions until persistence and fold reconstruction succeed; terminate lifecycle on commit failure.  
**Warning signs:** multiple public subjects emit before the transition promise resolves.

### Pitfall 8: Tests Reuse Implementation Arithmetic
**What goes wrong:** A test calls the helper under test to derive its expected common relay or edition hash and remains green with the same defect. [VERIFIED: v1.1 milestone audit]  
**How to avoid:** Hand-state relay sets and independently compute fixture hashes from the published CORD preimage or fixed known vectors.  
**Warning signs:** expected values import `evaluateCommonRelayCoverage`, `groupRotations`, or the resolver under test.

### Known Threat Patterns for Concord Rotation

| Pattern | STRIDE | Standard Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Disjoint ack sets make a complete rotation undiscoverable | Denial of Service | Common strict-majority intersection over normalized protocol relays. |
| Retry creates competing root at same epoch | Tampering / DoS | Persist-before-publish exact signed artifacts; one pending record per community. |
| Old/mismatched Grant citation authorizes a demoted actor | Elevation of Privilege | Exact pin resolution followed by current-roster bit/rank check. |
| Owner-shaped malformed citation bypasses canonical rules | Spoofing | Reject owner-with-`vac` before exemption. |
| Plaintext pending record leaks root/channel keys | Information Disclosure | NIP-44 self-encrypted, schema-versioned record over injected storage. |
| Relay text/ciphertext reaches logs | Information Disclosure / Repudiation | Stable IDs/enums only in logs; raw causes remain programmatic and protected. |
| Partial async store failure yields mixed adopted state | Tampering / DoS | Awaited staging/persistence and fatal transition failure. |
