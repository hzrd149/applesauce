# Phase 25.2 Plan 04: Live Rotation Convergence Summary

Serialized root and private-channel rotation convergence with bounded candidate-local reconciliation, down-only adoption, and typed non-fatal diagnostics.

## Accomplishments

- Added a shared coordinator that serializes classification and transitions after async store insertion completes.
- Adopted complete lower siblings immediately while ignoring equal and higher candidates through one live/historical down-only decision seam.
- Added deduplicated reconciliation for partial, inconsistent, continuity-gap, citation, and decrypt-blocked candidates, capped at three attempts within eight seconds.
- Routed private-channel live rotations through the same coordinator and merged channel diagnostics into the community-local observable.
- Kept remote candidate defects non-fatal while propagating store, persistence, and transition failures to the affected lifecycle error surface.
