# Phase 25.2 Plan 03: Durable Refounding Resume Summary

**Refounding now saves one authenticated, self-encrypted copy of every signed artifact before publication and resumes it through durable completion stages after restart.**

## Accomplishments

- Added a versioned pending-operation adapter that preserves typed arrays, validates restored identity and epoch data, and exposes no secret-bearing plaintext in storage.
- Threaded the client's shared `ConcordStorage` into every community and made material mirror persistence awaitable during Refounding adoption.
- Made mandatory publication, adoption, snapshot attempt, and cleanup independently resumable without rebuilding signed artifacts.

## Decisions Made

- Reused the signed root-rekey event id as rotation identity and stored the exact `RefoundingPlan`; no artifact or address is recomputed after restoration.
- Used a tagged JSON encoding for `Uint8Array` fields inside the authenticated NIP-44 envelope so reconstructed group keys remain byte-identical.
- Treated snapshot publication as best effort but persisted its attempt before transport, preventing a post-publish crash from replaying it.
