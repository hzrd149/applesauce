<objective>
Bump `nostr-tools` from `~2.19`/`^2.19` to `^2.24` in `packages/core`, `packages/common`, and
`packages/relay` (D-11, promoted from backlog 999.8), and pin the behavior change with a
regression test proving a plaintext over 65,535 bytes now round-trips through NIP-44.

Purpose: D-07 removes every serialized-byte cap in `packages/concord`. Installed nostr-tools
2.19.4 sets `maxPlaintextSize = 65535` and throws below the new limit, so removing concord's own
caps without this bump would just relocate the refusal into the library. This plan makes D-07
real rather than nominal. `packages/concord` never imports `nostr-tools` directly (grep-confirmed
zero matches) — it inherits transitively through `applesauce-core/helpers/encryption`, which is a
direct re-export.

Output: three bumped version ranges, an updated lockfile, and a behavioral test in
`packages/core`'s encryption suite.

Requirement attribution: this is folded scope with no REQ-ID of its own. It is filed under
WIRE-08 because WIRE-08's own requirement text is the only one that references the byte cap
("enforces the 50-membership protocol constant **alongside the already-enforced byte cap**"), and
D-07/D-11 are precisely what change "already-enforced byte cap" to "no byte cap".
</objective>
