<objective>
Migrate both engines and both sync walks off the client-wide registry and its ambient per-relay
drivers onto operation-scoped `onAuthRequired` handlers (D-01/D-02). Each `ConcordCommunity` and
each `ConcordPrivateChannel` constructs its own `StreamSigners`; every key-registration point keeps
its exact call shape but stops triggering anything; every read call site gains the scope's handler
beside the correctly-scoped `waitForAuth` it already passed.

This is the plan CAUTH-01 and CAUTH-02 turn on. It also removes four of CAUTH-03's five named
mechanisms from the engines (`authenticateStreamKeys` call sites, the refcounted drivers,
`ensureAuth()`, and registration-triggered authentication); the class carrying them is deleted in
plan 15-07 once its last two consumers migrate.

Purpose: today the *waiting* is already operation-scoped at every read site (`waitForAuth: authors`
is passed at all three), but the *authenticating* is client-wide — one shared registry handed to
every relay driver, re-firing every held key on every challenge. The relay already computes
`missingPubkeys` from the operation's own `waitForAuth`, so intersecting a scope-level signer map
against it is already operation-scoped with no per-operation key threading (D-02, the load-bearing
simplification of this phase).

Output: two engines and two walks with no proactive auth machinery, a green suite whose fixtures
no longer construct the doomed class, and the CAUTH-02 scoped-AUTH oracle.
</objective>
