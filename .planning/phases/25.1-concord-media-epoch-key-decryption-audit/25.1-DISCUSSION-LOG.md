## Compatibility Contract

| Decision | Alternatives considered | Selected |

**User's choice:** Preserve the wire format and current valid `encryption` property; add `rawImeta`, `encryptionError`, and descriptive error classes rooted at native `Error`.
**Notes:** The user explicitly rejected opaque error codes because they do not describe the failure. A new cross-package error hierarchy was considered and rejected as unnecessary scope expansion.
